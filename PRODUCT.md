# hack-calc

**One-line:** A razor-thin tribute to the Casio FX-82MS, built around an ESP32 and a 2.25" 76×284 ST7789 bar TFT.

## Audience
- Students learning electronics/embedded systems
- Retro tech & cyberdeck enthusiasts
- DIY makers who want a conversation-starting desk piece

## Goal
Sell physical kits (primary). Drive traffic to GitHub for DIY builders. Payments via Razorpay Payment Link once KYC clears.

## Core Features
- **Casio-style LCD**: Faithfully rendered dot-matrix display with DEG/M/STO annunciators
- **Porsche banner**: Moody 284×76 render of a Porsche 911 Carrera 4S
- **Cheat sheet**: Pocket trigonometry/algebra formulas
- **Smart backlight**: Per-state PWM dimming (active-low)
- **Open source**: Fully documented with wiring, patch, and build instructions

## Tone & Vibe
Brutalist/minimal. Monospaced typography, stark contrast, grid-based layout. Terminal-inspired without becoming a full CRT aesthetic.

## Constraints
- Physical product (needs shipping address)
- Open-source hardware project
- Razorpay Payment Link to be added post-KYC
