Shipping policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

topper.calc is made to order. This page explains where we ship, how long it takes, and what happens if a parcel is delayed or damaged.

1. Where we ship

We ship across India to serviceable PIN codes. We do not ship internationally yet. If your PIN code is not serviceable, we will contact you and refund the order in full.

2. Production time

Each device is assembled and tested after payment. Production takes about 2 weeks. We dispatch as soon as the unit passes testing.

3. Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 3-7 business days depending on your location and courier coverage. Remote PIN codes can take longer.

4. Charges

Shipping is included in the price of ₹6,499 shown on the product page. There is nothing extra to pay on delivery.

5. Tracking

When the parcel leaves us we email you the courier name and tracking number, and send updates to the phone number you provided at checkout. If you do not receive tracking within 3 business days of dispatch, write to us.

6. Delivery attempts

Couriers usually make two or three attempts. Please keep the phone number reachable. If the parcel is returned because the address was wrong or nobody was available, we contact you to arrange re-shipping at the actual courier cost.

7. Damaged in transit

If the outer box is visibly damaged, take photographs before opening and, if possible, refuse the parcel. If you only discover the damage after opening, email us within 48 hours at always.toppercalc@gmail.com with photographs and your order id. We will arrange a replacement or refund under the refund policy.

8. Delays

Courier delays, weather and local disruptions are outside our control. If your parcel has not arrived within the estimate, write to us and we will follow up with the courier. If it is lost, we will replace it or refund you in full.

9. Questions

Write to always.toppercalc@gmail.com with your order id. We reply within 1-2 business days.
