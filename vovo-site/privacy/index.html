Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use toppercalc.in or buy a device, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to orders placed through it.

1. Who is responsible

Nuke Labs, Kerala, India. For any privacy question or request, write to always.toppercalc@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Order details - name, email address, phone number and shipping address you provide at checkout.
  • Payment information - the payment id, order id, amount and status returned to us by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI id, CVV or bank credentials.
  • Support messages - what you send us by email, including attachments.
  • Technical data - the hosting provider logs basic request data such as IP address, browser and the page requested, for security and to keep the site running.

3. Why we use it

  • To take payment, confirm the order and produce the device.
  • To ship the device and share the minimum details a courier needs.
  • To answer support questions and handle returns or refunds.
  • To meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.

We rely on your consent and on the need to perform the contract you entered into with us. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us, but we may still need to keep records where the law requires it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Razorpay - to process the payment.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) - to serve this website.
  • Courier partners - name, phone and address, to deliver the order.
  • Email and support tools - to send order updates and reply to you.
  • Authorities - where we are legally required to disclose information.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Razorpay sets its own cookies on its payment pages; those are governed by Razorpay's privacy policy.

6. How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for as long as needed to support the order and to meet tax and accounting requirements. Support emails are kept for up to three years. You can ask us to delete other data at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete data we no longer need;
  • withdraw consent for future use.

Write to always.toppercalc@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to order data is limited to the people who need it to fulfil and support orders. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive documents by email unless we ask for them.

9. Children

The site is not directed at children under 18. If you are under 18, please place an order with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be notified by email to customers with an open order.
