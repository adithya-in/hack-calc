About topper.calc

Last updated: 1 November 2026

topper.calc is a scientific calculator with a second, wider display. The calculator works exactly like a normal calculator; the wide screen holds your own notes, formulas or reference images, and one button takes you back to the calculator.

We build it in small batches in India. Each unit is assembled by hand, tested, and shipped with the software already installed.

What it is for

It is a study aid: a place to keep the formulas, diagrams and reminders you would otherwise write on paper or scroll for on a phone. It is not a recording device, it has no camera or microphone, and it does not connect to the internet on its own.

What it is not for

It is not intended for use in examinations, tests or any setting where a second display or reference material is not allowed. Using it in an examination is against the rules of that examination and against our Terms. We do not support, encourage or take responsibility for such use.

How orders work

Orders are placed on this website and paid through Razorpay. We start production after payment and dispatch in about 2 weeks. You will receive shipping updates on the email or phone number you provide at checkout.
