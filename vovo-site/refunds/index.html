Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

We want you to receive a working device. This policy explains cancellation, replacement and refunds. It applies to orders placed on this website and paid through Razorpay.

1. Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel any time before the order is dispatched for a full refund. Email us with your order id; we will confirm the cancellation and start the refund.

2. Cancelling after dispatch

Once the parcel is with the courier it cannot be cancelled. If you refuse delivery, the parcel returns to us and we refund the amount minus the actual two-way courier cost once we receive it back in saleable condition.

3. Damaged or not working on arrival

Check the parcel when it arrives. If the device is damaged, does not switch on, or is not what you ordered, email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order id and photographs or a short video. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund, including any shipping you paid.

4. What is not covered

  • Damage caused after delivery, including drops, liquid damage and cracked displays.
  • Devices opened, soldered or modified by you beyond installing published firmware updates.
  • Change of mind after the device has been delivered and used.
  • Orders where the address or phone number provided was wrong and the parcel was returned.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay. We initiate the refund within 2 business days of approving it. Banks usually credit the amount in 5-7 business days; UPI refunds are often faster. You will get an email confirming the refund and a reference number you can use to track it.

6. Replacement

Where a replacement is agreed, we ship it after the original unit reaches us, or earlier if we ask you to keep it. Replacement units are tested before dispatch.

7. How to start a request

Email always.toppercalc@gmail.com with:

  • your order id or Razorpay payment id;
  • the date the parcel arrived;
  • what is wrong, with photographs or a short video.

We reply within 1-2 business days. If the device needs to come back to us, we email you the return address with the approval.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other law that applies to your order.
