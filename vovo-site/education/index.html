Education and study use

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Education is the reason topper.calc exists. We build a study aid for school and college students: a calculator shaped device with a wide second screen that holds the formula sheets, diagrams and notes a student would otherwise carry on paper or scroll for on a phone.

We also publish free browser based study tools - a scientific calculator, a unit converter, a quadratic solver, a percentage calculator and a searchable formula reference - for any student, whether or not they own the device.

What students keep on the screen

The wide screen holds one page at a time, about 288 characters or one full screen image. Students typically load:

  • Formula sheets for physics, chemistry and mathematics
  • Diagrams, labelled figures and circuit symbols
  • Periodic table extracts and common constants
  • Vocabulary lists, dates and timelines
  • Their own revision notes and reminders

Subjects

The device is subject agnostic. In practice it is used most for:

  • Physics - equations, constants, circuit and ray diagrams
  • Chemistry - periodic trends, reaction summaries, mole formulas
  • Mathematics - identities, standard results, geometry formulas
  • Biology - labelled diagrams and process summaries
  • Engineering - reference tables, conversion factors, standard values

Free study tools

Our study tools page has a scientific calculator with degree and radian modes, a length, mass and temperature converter, a quadratic equation solver with discriminant and vertex, a percentage calculator and a searchable formula reference covering maths, physics and chemistry. Everything runs in the browser, needs no account, and sends nothing to a server.

Academic honesty

topper.calc is a revision and reference aid, not examination equipment. It must not be used in examinations, tests or any setting where a second display or reference material is not allowed. Using it in an examination is against the rules of that examination and against our Terms. We do not support, encourage or take responsibility for such use.
